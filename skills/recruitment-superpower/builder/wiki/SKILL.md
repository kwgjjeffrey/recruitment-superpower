# Wiki preparation

Organize the requested knowledge in a local directory chosen by the user. Read only the material they supplied, retain its sources and prepare editable Markdown documents.

Wiki indexing, semantic registration and publication are not agreed yet. Do not create an index, claim a published wiki or call invented APIs. Report the local draft location and any questions that affect its organization. The Page and Table modules already have independent publication paths; neither is a substitute for a wiki index.
